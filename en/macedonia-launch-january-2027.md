Auravia Mobility is thrilled to announce our official premiere event in **January 2027**, marking the formal debut of Linktour electric vehicles in North Macedonia.

### What to Expect at the Event

Held in Skopje, this milestone event will gather industry leaders, urban mobility enthusiasts, and media partners for a firsthand look at the future of driving.

- **Unveiling the Lineup:** Full reveal of the flagship Linktour models engineered for smart urban travel.
- **Exclusive Test Drives:** Attendees will have the opportunity to schedule priority test drives ahead of market release.
- **Infrastructure Partnerships:** We will share key updates regarding our expanding ecosystem of fast-charging stations across the country.

### Be the First to Drive

Detailed venue information and VIP registration will open in late November. Stay tuned to our news feed or contact our team to ensure your spot on the guest list!
